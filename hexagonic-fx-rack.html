Inside the pack

Three different starting points for dark, detailed sound design.

  • Instrument Rack

    Dark Plucks

    This rack contains the pluck sound-design solution used in Stereo Voices In My Head. Its layered instrument setup includes an Erosion stage for extra texture.

  • Audio Effect Rack

    Shadow Creatures

    Parallel dry/clean and wet/texture chains. Auto Filter, Redux and Erosion are arranged to shape movement and rough-edged detail.

  • Instrument Rack

    Spectral Notes

    A Drum Rack-based instrument with two named layers: C1 Soft Ghost and D♯1 Tick Ghost, ready for ghostly accents and rhythmic detail.

Compatibility

Saved in Ableton Live 12.4.7–12.4.16 on macOS. Live 12.4.x or newer is recommended. Dark Plucks and Spectral Notes contain third-party VST3 instrument states, and some built-in devices may require Live Suite. Plug-ins and licences are not included; other systems have not been verified.
