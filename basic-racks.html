What do the Basic Racks do?

A quick guide to the seven racks in the pack.

  • Add Wides

    Adds stereo width with a separate mid path.

  • AnaWaves - Neuro Spectrum Split

    Divides the frequency spectrum into bands for separate processing.

  • Fat AF

    Combines saturation and multiband dynamics for added weight.

  • LTringliMutator

    Uses phasing, frequency shifting and drive for movement.

  • Move My Mid Harder Bandpass Version

    Focuses moving effects on a bandpassed midrange.

  • Move My Mids

    Moves the midrange while keeping a separate low path.

  • Real Split

    Routes high, mid and low bands to separate chains.

Descriptions are based on the rack files' structure; audio playback has not been independently tested here.
