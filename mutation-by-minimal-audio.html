Minimal Audio plug-ins required

These racks use Minimal Audio VST3 effects. To use the full pack, you need licensed copies of Rift, Morph EQ, Flex Chorus and Cluster Delay. Plug-ins and licenses are not included.

Inside the pack

Five distinct chains, each with six mapped macros.

  • Chimera

    Rift → Morph EQ → Rift. Two distortion stages around spectral movement.

  • Organism

    Morph EQ → Flex Chorus → Morph EQ. Two EQ shapes with a modulated chorus between them.

  • Phase Parasite

    Flex Chorus → Rift → Flex Chorus. Chorus motion around a central Rift stage.

  • Time Fracture

    Cluster Delay → Flex Chorus → Cluster Delay. Two delays with a moving chorus layer.

  • Spectral Civil War

    Morph EQ → Rift → Cluster Delay → Morph EQ. EQ movement, distortion and delay in one chain.
