Forsaken And Forgotten

7 October 2026

The story

The figures on the cover were once like you and me. Now they are long-forgotten, abandoned souls, reduced to a single mass. I believe they deserve a song.

And the moment they are awakened, they begin to scream into the void.

02 / 05

Chronicles Of Crisis And Salvation

20 June 2026

The story

This was the first track I imagined as AnaWaves. It went through around a hundred iterations, from the artwork to the other elements of the project, before it became this release.

Its story begins long after humanity is gone. An entity finds a fragment of our past: a piece of a vinyl record with only “Chrni Cri Salvtn” still legible. It becomes obsessed with discovering who made this object and will do everything it can to find the answer.

Tringli “Trinyó” László created the cover. It was inspired by the philosophical idea of being a brain in a vat, which Farkas Attila Márton (FAM), one of my influences, often talks about. My thanks to Trinyó and everyone who helped bring this work to life.

04 / 05

Let Me Tell It

7 April 2026

The story

This track started as a 2018 project called Selecta. After receiving a Virtual Riot sample pack, I tried to write drum & bass at 150 BPM using its sounds.

I returned to the project during Telekom Beats. Of everything I was working on, this was the track that let me express my anger about that experience most clearly.

05 / 05

Stereo Voices In My Head

24 January 2026

The story

The idea for this track began with a scribbled-over image that somehow made me think of my wife. In that moment, I knew I wanted her on the cover.

I tried to turn the voices in her head into music: the chaos, the order, and the space between them.
