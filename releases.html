01 / 05

Forsaken And Forgotten

7 October 2026

The story

The figures on the cover were once like you and me. I imagine they had separate lives and voices before they were abandoned and forgotten. Now even the boundaries between them have disappeared, leaving a single mass where there used to be individuals. That loss of identity is what I see when I look at the artwork. They cannot have their old lives back, but I believe they still deserve a song.

When they are awakened, they do not return quietly. They immediately scream into the void, as if everything that was buried with them has become audible at once. To me, the track belongs to that moment: the shock of waking, the sound of being left behind, and the possibility that someone might finally hear them.

N. Vanda designed the cover and gave these forsaken souls their haunting visual form. I wanted you to meet them there before pressing play.

02 / 05

Chronicles Of Crisis And Salvation

20 June 2026

The story

This was the first track I imagined as AnaWaves. It went through around a hundred iterations, from the artwork to the other elements of the project, before it became this release.

Its story begins long after humanity is gone. An entity finds a fragment of our past: a piece of a vinyl record with only “Chrni Cri Salvtn” still legible. It becomes obsessed with discovering who made this object and will do everything it can to find the answer.

Tringli “Trinyó” László created the cover. It was inspired by the philosophical idea of being a brain in a vat, which Farkas Attila Márton (FAM), one of my influences, often talks about. My thanks to Trinyó and everyone who helped bring this work to life.

03 / 05

WEAPONIZED

7 April 2026

The story

Before listening to WEAPONIZED, take a moment to look at the snail on the cover. To me, that is the place to begin. The image has a presence of its own: something familiar becomes strange enough to make you stop and look again.

I would rather leave some space around that image than explain every part of it. The cover can sit beside the music while each listener finds their own connection between the two. It is part of the experience of the track, not just a picture placed next to it.

Balazs Gal / NØIZ drew and designed the artwork. His imagination gave WEAPONIZED its striking visual identity. Thank you, Balazs, for this fantastic cover.

04 / 05

Let Me Tell It

7 April 2026

The story

This track began in 2018 under another name: Selecta. I had received a Virtual Riot sample pack and wanted to see what would happen if I tried to make drum & bass at 150 BPM using those sounds. It began as a production experiment, a way to find my own direction within a very specific set of materials.

During Telekom Beats, I opened the project again. Returning to that session changed what the track meant to me. I was angry about the experience, and of the music I was working on, this was the piece that let me express that anger most clearly. The sounds I had first explored years earlier now carried something much more personal.

For me, the release holds both moments: the curiosity of the original Selecta experiment and the frustration that brought me back to it. Let Me Tell It became a way to give that feeling a voice.

05 / 05

Stereo Voices In My Head

24 January 2026

The story

The first spark for Stereo Voices In My Head came from a scribbled-over image. I looked at it and somehow thought of my wife. I could not explain that association in neat terms, but I knew immediately that I wanted her on the cover. The visual idea came first, and it opened the way into the music.

I tried to imagine what the voices in her head might sound like and translate that inner world into a track. I wanted to make room for both chaos and order: thoughts overlapping and pulling apart, then briefly finding a shape again. The movement between those states became as important to me as either state on its own.

The cover and the song are inseparable in my mind. One image led me to think of her, and that thought gave the whole piece its emotional centre.
