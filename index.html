AnaWaves

AnaWaves is the independent project of Matt (Kovacs B. Mate), a Hungarian musician, producer and sound designer. He started out as a bass guitarist, guitarist and singer before moving into electronic music. Those roots still shape how he writes: even when a track is built with synthesis and effects, he wants it to carry the energy of a performance.

He makes neurofunk and drum & bass, drawn to strong rhythms, restless bass sounds and atmosphere. Sound design has become a central part of his work, especially FX racks. He spends a great deal of time building and testing chains of effects to discover what they can bring to a track. He is a fan of Puremagnetik and Minimal Audio, whose creative tools keep him curious.

AnaWaves has a visual side too. Matt commissions and pays artists to create cover art for his releases. He sees the images as part of each record's story, and values the people who help shape that world.

He plans to keep releasing music, collaborating with visual artists and sharing the FX racks and production resources he develops. He is also exploring Transfusion, an experimental idea about using the movement of one sound to shape another.
